Jimmy is an aspiring renaissance man. Graduating from Texas A&M University’s College of Architecture, he intends to conquer the worlds of gastronomy, viticulture, industrial design, fashion design, and pocket billiards. If it’s a dying art or a guilded craft, Jimmy Tran wants to learn it and master it. Somewhere in there he collects things and when there’s free time, it’s usually Suntory time.
