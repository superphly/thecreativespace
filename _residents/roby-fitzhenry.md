After his preadolescence foray into fine arts, Roby started designing at age 17 and went into branding and design full-time after graduating. After a short but successful stint at Texas A&M University, he decided to start Always Creative. At the ripe, young age of 26, Roby now serves as Creative Director of Always Creative, Desired Hearts and Rock The Republic. He is also an award-winning identity and print designer with an obsession for good web design.
