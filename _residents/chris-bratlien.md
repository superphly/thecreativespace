Chris began his programming career working in the CPI Insurance industry with Fields Financial Services, and then Teneo Software Solutions. He now collaborates with Always Creative, using WordPress, CodeIgniter, and various Javascript frameworks. Programming influences include Abelson and Sussman’s “Structure and Interpretation of Computer Programs” (SICP), as well as Alan Kay’s “The Computer Revolution Hasn’t Happened Yet”. Chris continues to both learn and unlearn “how” to program computers.
