Jeff graduated from The Art Institute of Houston in 2006. He spent a year working at the AIG headquarters in Houston. A year was all he could take of corporate America. He soon moved out to College Station, taking on the role Media Director at Always Creative. He makes time to play in an acoustic duo called Two Capitals and makes a hobby out of shooting and editing video.
