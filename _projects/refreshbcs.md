RefreshBCS was really the start of a lot of the events that came out of the Creative Space and around town. It was started in the Spring of 2007 and quickly grew from 30 or so members to just over 120. Refresh is a community of designers and developers working to refresh the creative, technical, and professional culture of New Media endeavors in their areas. Promoting design, technology, usability, and standards.
