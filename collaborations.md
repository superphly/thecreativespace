## ![refreshbcs](/media/2009/07/refreshbcs.jpg)

RefreshBCS was really the start of a lot of the events that came out of the Creative Space and around town. It was started in the Spring of 2007 and quickly grew from 30 or so members to just over 120. Refresh is a community of designers and developers working to refresh the creative, technical, and professional culture of New Media endeavors in their areas. Promoting design, technology, usability, and standards. ([http://refreshbcs.org](http://refreshbcs.org))

**Involves**: Everyone at the Creative Space

![Rock The Republic](/media/2009/08/rtr.jpg)

Rock The Republic (RTR) is NOT a festival; it’s a Spectacle of Sight and Sound! Rock The Republic is a three night event featuring the best up and coming musical acts, spoken word performers, and artists from the Lone Star State. Never before has an event so prominently fused the world of rock & roll with the dynamic slam poetry movement. And unlike many music “festivals” that rush bands on and off of stages, Rock The Republic will feature concert length performances allowing participants to fully enjoy their favorite artists. ([http://RockTheRepublic.com](http://RockTheRepublic.com))

**Involves**: Cody Marx Bailey, Roby Fitzhenry
